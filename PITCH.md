# HumanRelay — The Pitch

Voice rules: short declaratives, numbers we can defend, zero hype-words.
Lead with the thesis-as-news. Examples do the explaining.

## One-liner (10 seconds)

HumanRelay is the human layer for AI: when an agent or robot hits something its
model can't handle, our trained people answer in seconds — and every answer
becomes training data.

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

Every human knows not to drive into a flooded road. No dataset does — Waymo just
recalled 3,800 cars over exactly that. HumanRelay is the human layer for AI agents
and robots: an API that routes the questions models can't answer — *is this safe
to drive through? which pill is the heart medication? are these two listings the
same product?* — to trained, salaried professionals who answer in seconds, from
fifty cents a call. Every answer comes back as labeled training data, so the edge
case your fleet hits today is in your training set tomorrow. And we're built on
IndiVillage — 500 million annotations, 1,200 managed professionals — so one stack
covers your pretraining data, your annotation, and your 24/7 deployment fallback.

## The closer (workforce moat)

Gig platforms can't do this — you can't put anonymous crowdworkers behind a robot
in someone's kitchen. Salaried teams in offices we run, accountable by name.
That's why the accuracy holds and why your security review passes.

## Per-audience hooks (swap the middle example)

- **Humanoid / robotics COO:** "Deploy before the model is perfect: a trained
  operator behind every unit in the field, safety-gated teleop, and the
  intervention comes back as a demonstration for your next training run."
- **AV / delivery:** the flooded road + the contradicting construction cones.
  Anchor: the Waymo recall and four-city pause (Apr–May 2026).
- **Agent builders:** "Your model says 60% wasp. 60% isn't good enough when a
  dispatch rides on it. One API call, a human verifies, full audit trail."
- **Marketplace / retail catalog:** "Two listings, one UPC, different bottles —
  merge or split? You make millions of those calls a year; we sell the human
  adjudication layer as an API. Same teams already do this work at IndiVillage."
- **Home robotics:** the two white pills; the resident on the floor mid-workout.
  "The differentiator isn't acting — it's knowing when to hold."
- **Investors:** "Embodied AI's constraint isn't compute, it's human data and
  human judgment. We own the supply: capture, annotation, real-time judgment,
  teleop — one flywheel, already profitable behaviors at IndiVillage, and every
  intervention compounds the data asset."
