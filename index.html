Critical COGS at 13.9% vs 25–35% norm ▼
The cost of goods sold is roughly half the industry minimum. At a realistic 28% COGS rate, an additional €8,300 in costs would need to be accounted for over 15 weeks. No supplier quotes or breakdown by category (food vs beverage) is provided.
Critical No COGS breakdown by category (food vs beverage) ▼
Food typically runs 28–35% COGS, beverages 18–24%. Without a breakdown, it's impossible to assess menu pricing accuracy or identify margin leaks in specific categories.
Major No marketing costs budgeted ▼
Industry standard is 3–5% of revenue. For this plan's revenue, that's approximately €1,770–2,955 missing over 15 weeks. Critical for a new venue that needs to build awareness.
Major No insurance provision ▼
Public liability, employer's liability, property, and contents insurance are mandatory for hospitality. Estimated €200–300/month adds ~€750–1,125 over the period.
Major No owner compensation ▼
The business plan shows no salary or draw for the owner. This understates true operating costs and makes profitability appear closer than it actually is.
Major No tax provisions (Lithuania: 21% VAT, 15% corporate) ▼
VAT at 21% on revenue and 15% corporate tax are not budgeted. VAT alone could represent a significant cash flow liability even if reclaimable on inputs.
Moderate No depreciation on €36K fit-out assets ▼
€36,000 in fit-out costs should be depreciated over their useful life (typically 5–7 years for bar fit-outs), affecting both P&L and tax calculations.
Moderate No POS/card processing fees (1.5–3%) ▼
Card payment processing at 1.5–3% of revenue adds €886–1,773 over 15 weeks. With declining cash use, this is a material omission.
Moderate Staff wage figure ambiguous (gross vs net) ▼
If wages are stated as net, employer social contributions (30–40% in Lithuania) could add significantly to the actual cost. Needs clarification.
Moderate €100/week contingency is dangerously insufficient ▼
A single equipment failure or minor incident could exceed this. Industry best practice suggests maintaining at least 5% of revenue as contingency, approximately €270/week at current levels.
